AGRI-EYE TRIO K

Hybrid geospatial agricultural decision-support application

A desktop application concept that brings GIS, satellite remote sensing and drone GeoTIFF analysis into one agricultural workflow. It is designed to support field boundaries, crop monitoring, indicator comparison, candidate-zone screening and report export through both online and offline workflows.

  • Field mapping and GIS-oriented workspace
  • Satellite indicators and time-based comparison, where data are available
  • Drone imagery / GeoTIFF workflows and exportable reports
  • Screening indicators are proxies to support review, not definitive diagnoses

TERRADUX3K

Humanitarian Offline Drone Platform for Post-Conflict & Disaster Zones

Project vision: a 100% offline-first drone intelligence system for areas where internet fails and lives are at risk.

TERRADUX3K is a developing multi-use concept for drone-supported situational awareness in hazardous or hard-to-reach environments. The intended workflow combines local geospatial evidence, sensor observations and precise coordinates so trained teams can review candidate locations even without an internet connection.

Triple-use mission

01

Humanitarian deminingSupport candidate detection and mapping of possible landmines, unexploded ordnance (UXO) and chemical remnants for specialist engineering review.

02

Disaster responseHelp record candidate locations of trapped people or animals in areas affected by earthquakes, floods, rubble or blocked access.

03

Agri-safety surveyExplore and document land before cultivation to inform subsequent assessment and planning.

Development and safety note

TERRADUX3K is under development. It is not a certified mine-clearance or rescue system. Any sensor alerts are candidate indicators that require expert verification; the application must never be used alone to declare an area safe or to replace official emergency procedures.
